Within the Nonsuch Book Room ([open with the Service Wing](/opening-times)) we have a wide range of fiction authors from A to Z encompassing many genres such as crime, thrillers, "chick lit" and the classics. Our range of non-fiction is extensive and includes military and general history, the arts, crafts, sport, gardening, natural history, travel, cookery and poetry. We also have a good stock of children's and teenage titles. You may well find books you didn't know you wanted at very reasonable prices.

There is also a selection of fiction and non-fiction in the Loose Box which is available for purchase every Sunday when Nonsuch Palace Gallery is open from 12 noon to 4pm. New titles are added to our stock on a regular basis.

Cash and card payments accepted. All proceeds go to support the work of the Friends of Nonsuch.

Donations of good quality books are always welcome and can be left with the working party on a Thursday morning between 9.30am and noon. Alternatively I can arrange collection. Please phone me on [07847 074624](tel:+447847074624).

If you would like to help with the running of the Book Room please contact me on the mobile no above. No experience is needed so if you have a couple of hours to spare and enjoy the company of books why not get in touch?
